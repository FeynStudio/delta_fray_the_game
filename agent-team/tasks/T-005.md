# T-005 Respawn and turns: endTurn respawns destroyed ships and passes the turn after every move (Prototype 1, slice 5; second half of the turns request)

## Goal
After each move finishes, destroyed ships reappear at their spawn points and it becomes the other player's turn, with the power gauge ready on their ship; play never ends and there is no turn timer.

- Feature map: agent-team/FEATURE_MAP.md section 6 (Respawn & Turns, including Known traps: skipping a turn after a double kill, spawn protection), section 1 (debug API), section 4 (movement animation in src/input.js: startMove/stepMove and `state.anim`), section 9 (harness contract: `endTurn(state)`: "respawns destroyed ships at config.spawns[i] and passes the turn. game.js calls it after the move animation"; debug API: "step(ms) finishes the move animation and calls endTurn").

  - scenarios/turns.json vs expected/turns.json (run-scenario.mjs; inputs deep-frozen; scenario overrides spawns to (190, 480, 0) and (1090, 480, 180)): a destroyed enemy respawns at its spawn and the turn becomes 1 while the attacker stays at x 350; a ship that hits the wall respawns at (190, 480, 0) and the turn becomes 1; a double kill respawns both and the turn becomes 1 (not skipped); a plain move by player 2 passes the turn back to 0.
