## Coordinator interpretation and choices (not human decisions)
- "on top of all game mechanism so ship movement will not be impacted": the decal is drawn last in drawFrame (over map, ships, gauges and anything added later) and is decoration only: it is not in state, not in src/rules.js, and is not an obstacle; ships move, hit and die under it exactly as before.
- Coordinator's choice: "middle top of page" is drawn on the canvas at the top centre of the world (x = worldWidth / 2, a config offset below y = 0), so it scales with the map at every window size and stays inside the play area. At 1920 x 1080 this is also the top middle of the page.
- Coordinator's choice for "retro" and for keeping ships under it visible: capital letters in a monospace canvas font (no downloaded font, no new dependency), drawn as outline text (strokeText) in COLORS.dim, so a ship passing under it can still be seen. Text, font size, top offset and letter spacing are config values the human can tune on localhost.
