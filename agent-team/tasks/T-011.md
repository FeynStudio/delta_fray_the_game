  - Colourblind safety: the title is neutral grey and differs from the background by brightness, not hue. It cannot be confused with the blue and red ship colours.
  - Centring: the letters' visible centre is at x 637.3 instead of 640, about 3 px at 1080p. I cannot see it, so it is not an issue.
  - Scores: they stay in the corners. The title spans x 470 to 804, so even a much longer score would not reach it.
  - Ships: a ship under the letters stays visible in its own colour.
    - title-move-toward-title.png: the red ship inside the "A" is clearly visible.
    - title-ship-under-title-crop.png: the blue ship and the blue aim needle are clear across the space between the two words.
  - Blink: in title-start-blink-0ms, 100ms, 200ms and 300ms the title looks the same in each frame and stays at full opacity while the ships fade. The blink still reads.
  - Respawn: in title-respawn-explosion.png the explosion and the respawned ship are not affected.
  - Gauge: the power bar sits below the ship, under the title band, in every frame shown.
  - Visual style: the outline letters are "solid lines", matching the vector look and the map border, which uses the same grey. Grey is not a primary colour, but it is the existing interface grey, not a new hue.
  - Edge cases covered by tests: title drawn last in 4 kinds of frame, no filled text, save/restore so later drawing does not pick up the title's font or spacing, a config override is drawn, and the game state is unchanged.
- Issues:
  1. Direction dial under the title (title-ship-under-title-crop.png): the dial's arc and inactive parts use the same grey as the title (GAUGE_DIM is COLORS.dim). When a ship aims from the top-centre band (y about 16 to 64), the arc merges with the "A" and "F" strokes and its outline is hard to make out. The blue aim needle stays clear, so the player still sees where they aim. But in the most crowded part of the screen the dial's shape gets lost, and the title is drawn over it. This does not block approval. Possible fixes are a dimmer title or a different grey for the title only. That needs a human choice, so it is listed under Suggested ideas.
  2. Title brighter than blinking ships (title-start-blink-300ms.png): at blink opacity 0.25 the ships are faint while the full-brightness title is the brightest thing on the map apart from the scores. At every game start and respawn, the eye goes to the decoration rather than the ships. This is minor and a playtest question. It does not block approval.
  3. Small windows were not captured. Below about 700 px wide the title shrinks with the map; at a 375 px wide phone screen it would be about 14 px of thin outline. This is not a baseline concern. It is logged for awareness only and does not block approval.
- Suggested ideas:
  - Make the title fainter, for example a config value titleAlpha of about 0.5 to 0.6, while keeping it above 4.5:1. Or give the title its own grey distinct from the dial grey, so a ship aiming under the title keeps a readable dial. The BACKLOG T-011 observation already asks the human about this.
  - Optical centring: draw at x + titleLetterSpacing / 2. The 3 px offset is invisible, so this is low priority.
  - Later, a retro bitmap or vector font, needing no download, if the system monospace font looks too plain or differs across operating systems.
- Playtest questions:
  - In live play, does the title draw your eye away from the ships, especially during the spawn blink?
  - When your ship is near the top wall in the middle, can you still read the dial and the power bar, and judge your shot?
  - Does the title feel like part of the game or like a label stuck on it? Would you prefer it fainter, smaller or in mixed case ("Delta Fray")?
  - In Firefox or Safari, does the title look the same: spacing, font and centring? Only Chromium was captured.
