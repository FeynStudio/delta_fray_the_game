## Goal
On their turn, a player presses Space to lock a distance on a slowly sweeping power gauge, then a direction gauge appears (Shift flips it to backward), and a second Space locks the direction and launches with no confirm step.

## Decisions
(human answers only, one line each, also copied to agent-team/DECISIONS.md)
- Decided by human in session, 2026-11-08: "T-002 build the whole sequence"

Coordinator recommendation (owner may choose differently if it passes the Done-when checks; state the choice in the handoff):
- Sweep speed: start slow, about 2 seconds for the power reading to go from 0 to 1, and about 3 seconds for the needle to cross its 180 degrees. Both live in config so the human can tune them in playtest.
- Gauges sweep back and forth (0 to 1 to 0 for power; -90 to +90 and back for the needle).
- Shift toggles: a second Shift in the direction phase flips back to forward.
- Backward range: keep `needle` in -90..90 and let `backward: true` mean the needle is measured from the ship's tail instead of its nose; note the convention in a code comment for the movement slice.
