## Goal
On their turn, a player presses Space to lock a distance on a slowly sweeping power gauge, then a direction gauge appears (Shift flips it to backward), and a second Space locks the direction and launches with no confirm step.

  - Gauge shape: `{ phase: 'power' | 'direction' | 'moving', power: 0..1, needle: degrees relative to the ship's heading (0 = straight ahead), backward: boolean, distance }`; `distance = power × config.maxMove` once locked.
  - It sends real `keyDown` events with `code: 'Space'` (and once with `autoRepeat: true`) and reads `gauge.phase` after each.
  - The agent sandbox cannot start headless Chrome/Edge (BACKLOG, T-001). The browser half of the check needs a human run from a normal terminal, as in T-001.
- Stack and scope: plain JavaScript as decided by the human on 2026-11-07 (agent-team/DECISIONS.md). Ships stay slim outline-only triangles (decision of 2026-11-08).
