## Goal
When a player launches a backward move, the ship turns to face the direction it is about to travel (along the aimed path, not just straight back), flies there exactly like a forward move with its hit area following the turned ship, and keeps that new heading afterwards, so on its next turn the gauges sit at its new nose.

## Coordinator interpretation of answers 9 and Q-A (not human decisions)
- "flip the ship model first along the aim angle": at launch (the second Space), the mover's heading becomes the travel angle (heading + 180 + needle, wrapped), the same instant turn a forward move makes today. No animated rotation. Before launch, while aiming, the ship and the dial stay as T-002 built them.
- "then proceed as a forward movement": the move is swept, wall-checked and animated in the turned heading, exactly as a forward move with the same angle would be. Tip leads.
- "keep the new heading just like a forward move with updated direction": after the move the stored heading stays the travel angle, so applyMove treats backward and forward moves the same for heading. A backward move then differs from a forward move only in which half of the dial the player aimed with.
