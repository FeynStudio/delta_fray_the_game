## Goal
When a ship is destroyed (hit by an enemy's swept path or by reaching the wall), the player sees its triangle outline break into uneven pieces that fly outward and shrink to half size over 1 second, while play carries on without waiting.

## How the human's answer maps to this brief
- "play does not wait for explosion": the turn passes at the end of the move exactly as today; explosions are decoration that keep playing over the next turn.
- "triangle line breaking in uneven pieces and falls outward from ship's location": the destroyed ship's own outline triangle (same shape, heading and colour as drawn) is cut into line pieces of unequal length; each piece moves away from the ship's centre.
- "scale down 50% over 1 second": each piece's length goes from 100% to 50% over 1000 ms; then the explosion is removed. config.explosionMs = 1000, config.explosionEndScale = 0.5.
- "ship dies at wall's explosion appear inside the map": for a wall death the explosion is placed so the unbroken triangle lies fully inside the map.

## Coordinator choices (technical or tuning, not human decisions; all values in src/config.js so the human can tune them on localhost)
- Pieces: each of the 3 sides is cut once at a fixed uneven fraction from config (coordinator's choice: config.explosionCuts = [0.35, 0.6, 0.45]), giving 6 pieces. No Math.random, so tests are repeatable.
- Outward motion: each piece's midpoint moves straight away from the ship's centre at config.explosionSpeed game units per second (coordinator's choice: 40), at constant speed. Pieces keep their angle (no spin).
- Shrink: each piece shrinks about its own midpoint, linearly from scale 1 at 0 ms to config.explosionEndScale at config.explosionMs.
- Colour and style: the destroyed ship's colour (COLORS.ships[i]), line width config.shipLineWidth, outline only, no fade.
- Timing: an explosion starts in the step where the move animation ends (when the destroyed ship disappears, as today) and ages only through step(ms), so __game.step drives it in tests.
- Wall placement: the explosion centre is the mover's end position, moved the shortest distance needed so all three shipPoints at its heading are within 0..worldWidth and 0..worldHeight.
