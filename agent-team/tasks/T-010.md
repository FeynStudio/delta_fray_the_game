## Goal
While a ship is spawn-protected, its outline repeatedly fades away and comes back in quick succession, so players can see it cannot be destroyed; once it moves, it is drawn normally.

## Context
Request from the human, pasted as-is:
"add T-010 as ship outline gradually disapear but maintain in loop in quick sucsessions to simulate spawn protection animation."

- Depends on the T-006 rule (human decision 2026-11-09, A2). In normal play both ships are protected at the start of the game and every respawned ship is protected until its own next move, so with this task both ships blink at game start, and the active player's ship blinks while its gauges are showing.
- Current drawing (src/render.js drawShips, read 2026-11-09): outline only, full colour, no alpha. render.js reads state only and has no clock; src/game.js step(ms) is the only place time advances.
- Paved road: FEATURE_MAP section 8 (drawn element in src/render.js reading state only; tunables in src/config.js).

## Questions for the human (answered 2026-11-09, see Decisions)
1. Blink speed: one fade cycle lasts 400 ms.
2. Fade depth: fades to fully invisible (opacity 0).
3. Loop shape: fades out, then snaps back to full opacity and repeats.

## Coordinator choices (technical, not human decisions)
- Clock: src/game.js step(ms) adds ms to a state clock field (name chosen by the owner, added to FEATURE_MAP section 9 State). The blink is computed from that clock, so __game.step drives it in tests and render.js stays read-only.
- A pure `protectionAlpha(clockMs)` in src/render.js returns the opacity (0..1) for a protected ship; drawShips uses it only for ships where isSpawnProtected(state, i) is true, and restores full opacity for everything else.
- Cycle length and minimum opacity are config values (names chosen by the owner) set from the human's answers: 400 and 0.
- Coordinator's choice for "fade out then snap back": the fade is linear in time, from 1 at the start of each cycle down toward the minimum at the end, then back to 1 at the start of the next cycle: alpha = 1 - (1 - min) * ((clock mod period) / period). With min 0 this gives 1, 0.75, 0.5, 0.25 at 0, 100, 200, 300 ms and 1 again at 400 ms. The human can ask for a different curve on localhost.

### Design Review round 1 (2026-11-10)
- Design verdict: CHANGES NEEDED
- Checked: brief T-010; DESIGN.md (pillars, Visual style, Decided); DECISIONS.md (400 ms, fade to invisible, fade out then snap back, T-006 A2, zero-distance move ends protection); diff 23c1f28..b3f9d80 in src/config.js, src/game.js, src/render.js; isSpawnProtected / resolveMove / endTurn in src/rules.js; startMove / stepMove in src/input.js; draw order in drawFrame (map, scores, explosions, ships, gauge); blink-capture.json; I opened blink-start-0ms.png, blink-start-300ms.png and blink-after-p1-move-300ms.png myself; after-turns.log and after-all.log (RESULT: PASS on b098297). Result: the blink follows all three human answers. Only protected ships blink. Scores, power bar and direction dial stay at full colour. A moving ship never blinks. 2.5 flashes per second is under the WCAG 2.3.1 limit of 3 per second, and the flashing area is small. The signal does not rely on colour. No exploit: a protected ship can't be destroyed and is never a danger to the mover, so its invisible moments hide nothing that matters.
- Issues:
  1. Explosion plus respawn (evidence missing): when a ship is destroyed, it respawns in the same step its explosion starts. So for 1 s the new blinking ship and the old ship's pieces in the same colour are on screen together, sometimes close to each other. No frame shows this, and the drawShips test uses `explosions: []` even though the handoff says it covers explosions. Why it matters to the player: it's the busiest moment in the game, and the player has to be able to tell "my ship blew up" apart from "my new ship is safe". Asked for: one capture during a respawn turn with an explosion running and the respawned ship blinking (for example at 100 ms and 300 ms into the explosion), with entries in blink-capture.json.
  2. Fully invisible frame not shown: the captures stop at 300 ms (opacity 0.25), so the ship actually disappearing near the end of each cycle (about 380–399 ms) is never shown. Why it matters to the player: for about 10–25% of every cycle a protected ship, including the active player's own ship in the power phase, is barely visible or gone. Asked for: one frame at 390 ms or so, showing that the power bar and the direction dial still mark where the active ship is.
  3. Real-time look not recorded: there are only fixed headless frames at 100 ms steps, with no recording at real frame rate. The hard snap back (a hard cut, while Visual style asks for "smooth sliding of ship") and the comfort of 2.5 flashes a second can only be judged in motion. Why it matters to the player: flicker comfort and readability. Asked for: a short real-frame-rate recording of game start or a respawn, or a human localhost check recorded in the brief before approval.
- Suggested ideas:
  - Start each ship's blink when it respawns, not on the shared game clock. Right now a respawned ship can appear at about 0 opacity on its first frames, so it pops in invisible. This is a timing detail, not a change to the human's curve.
  - A first-time hint (a short line or a legend) explaining that a blinking ship can't be destroyed until it moves. Today nothing on screen teaches the A2 rule.
  - Optional reduced-motion or accessibility setting that swaps the blink for a steady faint outline when `prefers-reduced-motion` is set.
- Playtest questions:
  - When your own ship is fading during the power phase, do you lose track of where it points before the direction dial appears?
  - After a kill, can you instantly tell the explosion apart from the respawned blinking ship of the same colour?
  - Does the hard snap back feel like a retro invulnerability blink, or like a glitch or a strobe? Is 400 ms comfortable over a long session?
  - Do first-time players work out on their own that a blinking ship can't be hit, and that moving ends it?
- REPEATS: Evidence claims that are bigger than the tests: the handoff says the drawShips states "include explosions", but the explosion arrays are empty. Same kind of gap as earlier rounds where Not verified items needed a direct capture.
- Promote to guard: A unit test or guard that keeps the flash rate at 3 a second or lower, i.e. `config.protectionBlinkMs >= 334` while `protectionMinAlpha` is below about 0.5, so a later tweak can't push the blink over the WCAG 2.3.1 limit.
- Risk for the human: You chose "fade to invisible", so for part of every 400 ms cycle the ship's outline falls well below the usual 3:1 contrast for shapes (WCAG 1.4.11). I respect that decision and am not reopening it. If players with low vision or on dim screens lose their ship, the change is `protectionMinAlpha` (for example 0.25) in src/config.js. Also: protection and the blink both ignore time, so a ship that is never moved blinks for as long as it isn't moved. That follows the A2 rule (time limits are out of scope) and is worth watching in playtests.
