# T-003 Movement: the second Space flies the ship the locked distance and direction (Prototype 1, slice 3)
Status: IN REVIEW

## Goal
After the second Space, the active ship flies in a straight line, over a short animation, the distance locked on the power gauge in the direction locked on the direction gauge (forward or backward), ends where rules.applyMove says, and the gauges come back for the next move.

## Decisions
(human answers only, one line each, also copied to agent-team/DECISIONS.md)
- Decided by human in session, 2026-11-08: "refuse the move and leave ship where it is."
  Context: answers the coordinator's question about a move longer than maxMove (refuse vs. shorten to maxMove).

Coordinator recommendation (owner may choose differently if it passes the Done-when checks; state the choice in the handoff):
- Animation: a fixed time per move, about 600 ms, in config (for example `moveAnimMs`), with constant speed along the segment. A forward move turns the ship to its new heading when the move starts.
- Keep the animation's progress in the state object game.js owns (an extra field is fine; getState/setState copy it), so `step(ms)` and the tests can drive it.
- In the handoff, list as "Not verified" anything only a real browser shows (smoothness, the look of the move), unless the human checks it on localhost.
